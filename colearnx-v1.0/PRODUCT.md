# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Learners purchase and access training; Trainers create and manage course offerings; Creators publish paid digital resources; Administrators govern accounts, content, and financial workflows. Each role works in an authenticated task flow and must see only the records and actions relevant to that role.

## Product Purpose

CoLearnX is a controlled training marketplace that connects learning, course delivery, and paid digital content through a server-authoritative workflow. Success means users can confidently create, review, buy, and access the correct product without role confusion, data leakage, or ambiguous state.

## Brand Personality

Professional, clear, friendly. The interface should feel calm and accountable: it explains status, protects consequential actions, and keeps users moving through their current task.

## Anti-references

Avoid role-blended dashboards, hidden state changes, destructive actions without explicit confirmation, jargon-heavy developer-facing copy, and decorative UI that distracts from operational tasks.

## Design Principles

1. Role context first: label and filter each workspace by the user's current job.
2. State is explicit: missing, draft, submitted, published, and deleted states must be understandable at a glance.
3. Safe progress: use inline guidance and confirmation for consequential actions; preserve a clear path to recover from mistakes.
4. Server truth: show only metadata and actions authorized by the backend; never imply a local state is final.
5. Familiar controls: retain consistent buttons, icons, feedback, and keyboard-visible focus across product pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-accessible controls. Do not rely on color alone for status; use clear labels and icons. Respect reduced-motion preferences and use concise, plain-language error messages.
